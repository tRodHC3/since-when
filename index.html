Rules of thumb

  • Animal rennet or unspecified enzymes in cheese with no source → ANIMAL ENZYME. Do not guess slaughter status.
  • Microbial / FPC / vegetable rennet with a cited manufacturer or certifier statement → COMPATIBLE for that SKU after effective_on. MARKED only if the pack logo is sourced.
