ANIMAL RENNET

Rennet is the enzyme that turns milk into cheese. Animal rennet is taken from a slaughtered calf’s stomach. Dhabiha rules say that calf must be alive when the throat is cut. A prayer is part of the rite. Some plants stun first. Some do not. The source does not say which. If you do not want a religious slaughter in your cheese, this is the SKU to skip.

Two dates

One date is when the recipe changed. The other is when the box started saying so. They are usually not the same date. The gap is the part nobody tells you.

Who said it

A company FAQ is not a certifier mark. A certifier mark is not a government grade. This app prints which one it found, and links it.

Date unspecified

The record has no date for that change. The app prints that instead of guessing. A missing date is a fact about the record, not about the food.

Slaughter

Dhabiha is the slaughter rule: throat cut while the animal is alive, invocation said over it. That is a religious requirement, not a USDA flavor grade. Not every marked product is meat. Lab-enzyme cheddar is a certification claim. Animal-rennet cheddar is a slaughtered calf. This app prints the source, not a fatwa and not a speech.
