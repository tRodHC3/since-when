# Notes

- Haribo US vs Turkey is TWO barcodes, not one status. Do not assume a single Haribo verdict covers both markets.
- Jet-Puffed US pork gelatin needs the pack. Don't assume a US Jet-Puffed marshmallow is PORK without a photographed pack or a sourced ingredient list.
- Cabot shredded/sliced cheese is often excluded from IFANCA certification even when the blocks are certified. Check the specific SKU, not the brand.
- Do not invent GTINs here. A missing barcode means the row is skipped by `propose_seed.py`, not guessed.
